# Northstar Studio — Example Handbook

This is fictional sample data for testing the document assistant.

## Working hours
The team works Monday through Friday, 9:30 AM to 5:30 PM IST.
Employees may work remotely on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

## Leave policy
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 7 days before the planned start date.
Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days.

## Customer refunds
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase.
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 business days.
To request a refund, customers must provide their order number and purchase receipt.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days. Express shipping takes 2 business days.
Orders above INR 2,000 qualify for free standard shipping within India.

## Support
Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST.
Urgent incidents should be reported through the internal incident portal.
